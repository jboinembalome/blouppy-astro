# Topic illustrations

Three illustrations per topic (`src/constants/topics.ts`), each in a light and a
dark version. A post picks one with `illustration` in its front matter (1 to 3);
neighbouring posts of the same topic use different ones.

Source: [unDraw](https://undraw.co), free to use without attribution under the
[unDraw licence](https://undraw.co/license). The accent is set to Blouppy's
indigo (`#676dcf`, `#8e93dc` in dark), and in the dark version the near-black
figures become light slate and the whites become dark slate, so they stay
visible on a night background.
